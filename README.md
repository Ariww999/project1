# 🍋 Lemonade Dreams - Interactive 3D Narrative Game

An emotional, choice-driven narrative game about a little girl selling lemonade to buy her dream bicycle.

## 🎮 Game Overview

This is an interactive 3D narrative experience where your choices matter. Meet a cheerful little girl running a lemonade stand and discover how your purchasing decisions impact her dreams.

## ✨ Features

- **Fully 3D Environments**: Beautiful 3D scenes built with Three.js
- **Branching Narrative**: Two different outcomes based on your choices
- **Interactive Dialogue System**: Engaging conversations with meaningful choices
- **Dynamic Quantity Selection**: Choose how many lemonades to buy
- **Emotional Storytelling**: Experience a heartwarming (or heartbreaking) story
- **Restart Function**: Replay to explore different outcomes
- **Responsive Design**: Works on desktop and mobile browsers

## 🎯 How to Play

1. Open `index.html` in any modern web browser
2. Read the story and make your choices
3. Decide how many lemonades to buy (hint: your choice matters!)
4. Experience Day 2 and see the consequences of your actions
5. Click the Restart button to try different choices

## 🔀 Story Branches

### Path 1: Buy 5+ Lemonades
If you buy 5 or more cups of lemonade, you'll help the little girl achieve her dream! The next day, you'll see her with her new bicycle, filled with gratitude and joy. 🚲✨

### Path 2: Buy Less Than 5 Lemonades
If you buy fewer than 5 lemonades, the little girl falls short of her savings goal. The next day, you'll find her in tears, unable to afford the bicycle she's been dreaming about. 💔😢

## 🛠️ Technical Details

- **Built with**: Pure HTML, CSS, and JavaScript
- **3D Engine**: Three.js (r128)
- **No dependencies**: Single file, self-contained
- **Browser compatibility**: Works on all modern browsers with WebGL support

## 🎨 3D Scenes Include

- Lemonade stand with pitcher and cups
- 3D character model of the little girl
- Trees, flowers, and environmental details
- Bicycle model
- Weather effects (sun, clouds, rainbow)
- Dynamic lighting and shadows

## 💡 Design Philosophy

This game explores themes of:
- **Compassion and Kindness**: Small acts can have big impacts
- **Consequence of Choices**: Your decisions affect others' lives
- **Dreams and Determination**: The importance of supporting others' goals
- **Emotional Engagement**: Creating meaningful interactive narratives

## 🎬 Gameplay Tips

- Take your time to read the dialogue
- Consider the girl's story before making your purchase
- Try both paths to experience the full narrative
- Use the restart button to explore different outcomes

- **Mouse**: Click buttons to make choices
- **Quantity Selector**: Use +/- buttons to adjust lemonade count
